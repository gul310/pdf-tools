# PDF1998 — 100% Free & Private Online PDF Suite

Every PDF Tool You Need. Zero Server Uploads.

Convert, sign, edit, encrypt, and manipulate your sensitive PDF documents directly in your web browser. Powered by WebAssembly (QPDF) and client-side processing, your confidential files never leave your device memory.

## Key Advantages

- **Total Data Privacy**: Zero files uploaded or saved to remote servers. All processing runs locally in browser memory.
- **Blazing Fast Speed**: Zero upload/download waiting times. Uses your computer's native processor.
- **QPDF WebAssembly Engine**: Industry-grade C++ PDF engine compiled to WASM for bulletproof reliability.

## Privacy Architecture

Standard web-based PDF converters upload your contracts, financial reports, tax returns, and presentations to remote third-party cloud servers where they can be retained, inspected, or leaked in server breaches.

PDF1998 is engineered on a different paradigm: **100% browser-side processing**. Every conversion, extraction, signature, crop, annotation, and 256-bit AES encryption operation happens completely inside your local web browser tab memory. You can even disconnect your internet after loading the page and all 20 tools continue functioning smoothly.
